Computational periscopy · one photo · one shadow

Varjoluotain

A camera sees only a blank wall. Around the corner a screen lights that wall, and a 7.5 cm plate halfway between them casts a soft shadow. From one photo of the wall, this page reads the screen back. Drag the plate, change the picture, or take the plate away.

Of the 432 independent ways the screen can change, these many move the photo by more than the camera's noise. The rest are invisible: two screens that differ only there give the same photo, and the reconstruction fills them in from its smoothness prior.

Why a shadow helps

Without the plate, every patch of the screen lights the wall with the same broad glow, so their contributions blur into each other. The plate's shadow edge lands on a different part of the wall for each patch. Each patch leaves its own fingerprint, and the photo can tell them apart. The plate is a probe, the same idea as the small test tables in Luotain.
